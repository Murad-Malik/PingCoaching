Share your screen

Your viewer will be able to ping it, and you'll see the marker appear right on top of whatever you're doing.

This is the small relay server, not a screen-share provider. The default points at a shared always-on server - change it only if you're running your own.

Share your whole screen, or just one application - a specific game or browser window, say - so nothing else on your desktop is ever visible.

Higher quality looks sharper but uses more bandwidth and CPU. Your viewer can still choose to watch at a lower quality than this.
